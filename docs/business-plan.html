1 · Storyline + Storyboard

TAG CITY's own story — the resistance tags the surveillance city. This is not SIGNAL RUN's story; nothing from it is carried over.

The premise

The city runs on Sentinel-7 — quad-rotor industry drones with red scan eyes that sweep the alleys, reading walls for unlicensed color. The industry paints everything grey and calls it order. But color is evidence that someone was alive here. A crew of five anonymous artists — THE ARTIST, PRISM, GHOST, HEIR, PIXEL — moves through the alley sectors at night, hitting wall after wall with neon murals before the drones' searchlight cones lock on. Every completed tag is a small act of proof: we were here, and the city is ours tonight.

All characters are masked, stylized originals (art-bible §10). The crew's faces are never shown — anyone can be the artist.

3 · Gameplay Loop

Run → chase → spray-tag. One session = one alley sector; the loop never stops moving — standing still is being caught.

Core loop

  1. RUN — auto-run hero through the sector; 8-frame aggressive run cycle (hard forward lean, jacket lagging one frame). Player controls: swipe to dodge, tap for jump (anticipation crouch → cyan spark pop), slide (low crouch, can scraping ground, cyan spark trail), dash (0.25s max, full lean + cyan ribbon streaks).
  2. CHASE — Sentinel-7 drones escalate through three readable threat stages (bible §3): Patrol (eye dim, minimap red dot) → Suspicious (eye brightens to `#FF3B4E`, searchlight cone sweeps, minimap dot pulses) → Lock-on (eye flares white-hot, beam narrows, "DRONE DETECTED! INCOMING • EVADE" red-dashed alert, minimap red ring). Players dodge the cone, not the drone. Near-miss on the beam = white→gold impact flash + combo points.
  3. SPRAY-TAG — hold to spray on tagged walls; dual streams (cyan left, magenta right) from the hero's signature pose. Tag completes → impact splat bakes in (hue outline + fill + 4–8 drips + satellite droplets), wall blooms grey→neon over ~0.5s (pre-rendered crossfade frames, never a shader), gold burst + gold rain on milestones.

Combo + rewards

  • Combo meter — magenta flame/x-badge treatment (bible §5.6): "COMBO x5, MULTIPLIER 1.5x" in drippy cyan badge frame. Badge scale-pops +1 per tier in discrete steps — continuous per-frame scaling is killed (§7).
  • Reward language — gold rain (falling gold sparks, 0.8s) on prize drops and combo milestones. Gold is the earned channel ONLY (§1 rule 3).
  • Paint economy — paint meter drains as you spray; refill events = cyan bucket icon + pink "+30 REFILL". Running dry mid-tag breaks your combo — resource pressure without paywalls.
  • Roster progression — THE ARTIST + PRISM (final turnaround) playable at launch; GHOST, HEIR, PIXEL unlockable through progression. Each member: unique tool silhouette, unique visor, unique run cadence — readable at 64px.

5 · Music / Prize / Credit Economy

Twenty Minds verdict recorded below. Economy decision: (A) free-to-play fun-points credits + listening-gated streaming-link prizes — the Neon Credits design. No premium/IAP lane.

Neon Credits design (Black's spec, 2026-09-20 — applied to TAG CITY)

  • The prize name + credit score display on the game itself. The screen lights up for a jackpot.
  • Every win banks credits — fun points only, no cash value, music links only. Never gambling in any form (Black's hard line).
  • Jackpot = 1,000 credits = pick any music platform instantly.
  • After every win: "Cash in now, or save as credit?" — saved credits persist between visits (device-local; cross-device persistence is a backend we do not have — see Gaps).

Credit wallet

The optimization ladder (never a kill path)

Production law (Black, verbatim): "The look ship exactly because of the team, nothing dies if you want it to live." If the tech can't hold the look at 60fps, the team finds the way — precedent sweep first (who solved this and how), then build the missing tool, route around the wall, optimize deeper. The ladder's rungs, each preserving the exact look:

  1. Rung 1 — shrink glow footprint: fill-rate, not JS, is the binding constraint (small-sprite control recovered 59.7fps at 400 entities). Tighter glow radius, same look.
  2. Rung 2 — re-engineer the rain layer: rain is pure atmosphere; re-bake it cheaper (fewer draws, same two crossed streak layers) — never delete it.
  3. Rung 3 — re-engineer the parallax layer: the largest per-frame bg cost; cache harder, redraw less — the depth stays.
  4. Rung 4 — precedent sweep + build the missing tool: nothing we hit is new under the sun; research how it was done, retrieve the answer, apply it, turn it into a runbook.

A cheapened 30fps ship is forbidden. There is no branch where the lane dies over a perf miss — gate-review §5's old kill branch is SUPERSEDED by Black's correction (PRODUCTION-RULES.md rules 2, 8).
